Not deployed Full-stack web app

Timekeeper

A workforce time-tracking application. Employees sign in with their work Microsoft account, start a shift and switch between activities like productive work, breaks, lunch and active pauses, while the app compares their day against the scheduled plan.

  • Live timers with per-activity limits and over-limit alerts
  • Plan vs. actual timeline drawn from the daily schedule
  • Offline-first: records queue on the device and sync in batches
  • Server-validated data and clock correction against the server time

Aegis Workshop

A drag-and-drop editor for designing websites visually. Users place components on a canvas, fine-tune every style property, add interactive behavior and export the result as clean, standalone HTML and CSS.

  • No-code actions: triggers, conditions and animation presets
